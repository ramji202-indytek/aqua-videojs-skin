Design thinking

Video controls usually disappear into the picture. AQUA gives them a place to live: the strip is the front glass of a small tank, and the playhead is a fish that swims along the timeline.

Motion carries the meaning. Fish, bubbles, kelp and the water surface only move while the video plays, and they freeze in place on pause, so the screen is calm exactly when you reach for a control. The controls themselves sit on frosted-glass pills with dark tint and bright text, so they stay readable over any footage.

AQUA is deliberately distinctive, made for sites where the video is the fun part: aquariums, kids’ content, wellness and travel. It is not a neutral skin. Three water themes (Reef, Lagoon, Deep) recolour the whole scene, and a few CSS variables set the rest.

Everything is built from Video.js v10 components (@videojs/html) with plain CSS. Speed, quality, audio track and caption language sit in Settings; captions, clock, channel logo and loop have their own buttons in the bar.

  • Keyboard Tab through every control, arrows seek, Esc closes menus.
  • Touch 44px targets and double-tap to skip.
  • Motion With reduced motion on, nothing animates at all.

Controls

In the bar: play and pause, stop, back and forward 10 seconds, mute and volume, the seek bar with the fish playhead, the time, then captions, clock, channel logo and loop toggles, a stereo meter, Settings and fullscreen. On narrow players the four toggles move into Settings.

Accessibility

  • Readable Frosted glass with a dark tint and bright text keeps icons and times legible over bright or dark footage.
  • Tappable Every control is at least 44 by 44 px.
  • Focus A solid 3 px white ring with a dark halo on every control.
  • Keyboard Everything is reachable by Tab, menus close with Esc and focus returns to the Settings button.
  • Calm With reduced motion on, nothing animates. The fish and bubbles are decorative and hidden from screen readers.
  • Checked An automated axe scan of this page found no violations at desktop and phone sizes.

Use it on your site

Deploy this page, then drop it into any site with one iframe. embed=1 hides everything except the player, and src sets the video (a Mux stream, an .mp4 or a YouTube link).

<iframe src="https://YOUR-SITE/?embed=1&src=https%3A%2F%2Fstream.mux.com%2FPLAYBACK_ID.m3u8"
  title="AQUA video player" allow="autoplay; fullscreen" allowfullscreen
  style="width:100%;aspect-ratio:16/9;border:0"></iframe>

From source: npm install, npm run dev, then npm run build for a static site.
